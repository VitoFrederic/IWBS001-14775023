A short history of the garden

North Sydney Community Garden began with a group of 10 people gathered at Wendy Whiteley's Secret Garden in Lavender Bay. Their idea was simple, to create a space where all people from all ages could come together, grow fresh food, and build friendships. The garden was created for all people who enjoy gardening, connect with nature and create a community where people could share knowledge, support each other, and enjoy outdoor activities together. Today, North Sydney Community Garden (NSCG) has become one of the largest garden community in Sydney.

What do we do?

At North Sydney Community Garden, we do a lot of activities such as gardening, learning, and understanding more about nature.

Growing & Plant Care

We grow a wide variety of fruits, herbs, flowers and vegetable. Member learn how to planting, watering, maintaining and harvesting.

Protecting environment

Besides of gardening, we also encourage to protect our environment by introduce composting, recycling and water convservation. By that, we help create a help create healthy environment for next generation.
